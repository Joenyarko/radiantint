Presentation Impact Sectors

Four Core Pillars of Grassroots Impact

Direct institutional sectors from the Radiant Trust International presentation profile addressing deep social challenges.

01 • Agriculture
01

Agriculture & Sustainable Food Systems

Promote sustainable agro-based solutions to improve the livelihood of the rural poor, providing practical skills training and starter implements for agribusiness enterprises across Ghana.

  • Agro-tool distributions & starter kits
  • Post-harvest solar drying technologies
  • Smallholder farmer cooperative resilience
02 • Education
02

Education & Technical Vocational Training

Work directly with communities, teachers, and parent associations to improve educational standards, youth literacy, and TVET vocational skillsets for underprivileged young men and women.

  • Youth TVET apprenticeship toolkits
  • Vocational master trainer mentorship
  • Rural youth entrepreneurship hubs
03 • Clean Water
03

Clean Water Boreholes & Sanitation

Enhance rural livelihoods through the strategic expansion of clean, solar-powered potable water boreholes and community-wide hygiene and sanitation infrastructure across deprived regions.

  • Geophysical groundwater surveys
  • Automated solar-powered boreholes
  • Community water management committees
04 • Healthcare
04

Healthcare & Humanitarian Relief

Deliver targeted compassionate assistance, medical outreach, and substance recovery counseling to vulnerable populations, including persons fighting drug addiction and physically challenged citizens.

  • Substance recovery & rehabilitation guidance
  • Assistive aid for physically disadvantaged
  • Grassroots community health screenings
