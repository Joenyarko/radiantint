About RTI Ghana

Pioneering Social Enterprise & Humanitarian Action

Radiant Trust International (RTI) Ghana is dedicated to eradicating hardship and promoting social and environmental change in deprived areas of Ghana and West Africa.

Our Identity & Mandate

A Ghanaian Social Enterprise Transforming Grassroots Realities

Radiant Trust International (RTI Ghana) is an indigenous non-governmental humanitarian organization and registered social enterprise operating from its national secretariat at 14 Mango Avenue, Tantra Hills, Accra.

Operating across all 16 administrative regions of Ghana, RTI actively collaborates with public sector entities, international funding agencies, and likeminded non-profits to address root developmental challenges. Our interventions focus on sustainable agro-ecological food systems, educational standards enhancement, technical and vocational training (TVET), clean solar borehole water, and targeted humanitarian relief.

We believe that long-term social progress occurs when rural communities and local leaders possess the tools, technical skills, and institutional partnerships to cultivate their own sustainable livelihoods.

"RTI envisions a society where deviant behaviors or conducts commonly considered inappropriate in our society are eradicated or reduced to the barest minimum through empowerment, self-reliance, and human dignity."

Strategic Blueprint

Vision & Mission

Our forward-looking compass guiding every intervention, community engagement, and regional partnership.

OUR VISION

Become a leading social enterprise in Ghana and the West Africa subregion, recognized for grassroots impact, sustainability, and transformative community uplift.

OUR MISSION

Help build a society devoid of hardships through the promotion of agro-ecological principles, health, education, and rural entrepreneurship through capacity development and South-South exchange programmes.

Humanitarian Mandate

Our 10 Key Activities

From donor mobilization to rural agricultural development, discover the ten concrete ways Radiant Trust International delivers grassroots impact.

1

Aid & Support Mobilization

Collaborate closely with funding agencies and international development partners to provide humanitarian aid and targeted relief to deprived communities.

2

Educational Standards Enhancement

Work directly with communities, teachers, and parent associations to promote and improve educational standards, literacy, and school facilities.

3

Government & Non-Profit Partnerships

Forge sustainable partnerships with Government institutions and other non-profit entities to solve social and environmental challenges confronting underprivileged citizens.

4

Agribusiness Skills & Start-Tools

Provide comprehensive skills training and starter tools/implements for individuals venturing into agribusiness and other economically viable enterprises.

5

Humanitarian Aid for Disadvantaged

Provide compassionate humanitarian assistance to vulnerable groups including persons suffering from drug addiction and physically disadvantaged individuals.

6

Community Research & Solution Models

Conduct empirical research into social and economic problems in deprived communities and design effective, replicable models of solutions.

7

Sustainable Agro-Based Solutions

Promote organic, climate-smart agro-ecological solutions to improve the livelihood, self-sufficiency, and nutrition of the rural poor.

8

Agricultural & Water Supply Systems

Enhance rural livelihoods through the strategic expansion of agricultural development, clean borehole water supply, and sanitation facilities.

9

Vocational & Technical Training (TVET)

Champion and finance technical and vocational skills training for the less privileged, unlocking sustainable, high-earning career paths.

10

Climate Change Action & Agro-Ecology

Promote environmental preservation, tree-planting, climate-adaptive farming, and ecological education to safeguard communities against global warming.

Our Ethical Code

Values We Stand For

At Radiant Trust International, every intervention is steered by five foundational principles that ensure lasting social impact, community ownership, and transparency.

01

Transparency

Uncompromising openness in all resource management, donor allocations, and project reporting across Ghana's 16 regions.

02

Trustworthy

Building long-term, dependable relationships with partner communities, donors, funding agencies, and local governments.

03

Teamwork

Fostering unity and inter-disciplinary collaboration across all 16 diverse administrative regions of Ghana and West Africa.

04

Accountability

Strict governance, rigorous independent audits, and answering directly to the communities, partners, and citizens we serve.

05

Reliability

Consistent, high-impact humanitarian delivery that transforms rural livelihoods and ecosystems for generations to come.
